# ChessDesk v0.1.0

Initial versioned release of ChessDesk.

## Included

- Coach accounts and per-coach records for individual students and school clients.
- Session scheduling and status updates for online and in-person coaching.
- Invoice, payment, and business expense records, with dashboard summaries.
- FastAPI API, Next.js frontend, and SQLite or PostgreSQL persistence.
- OpenTelemetry traces and metrics, plus a local Prometheus, Tempo, Loki, and Grafana stack.
- AWS development and production deployment infrastructure with GitHub Actions.

## Known limits

- Session notification events are recorded in the app, but WhatsApp and email delivery providers are not connected.
- Password reset requests do not send email.
- Demo dates are fixed examples and can become stale.
